# Third-party and bundled asset licences

This lists each asset under `assets/` and the licence the upstream project
(Gilleece/moonlight-android-xr, `README.md` "Licences" section and the
`NOTE.txt` files under `tools/`) states for it. Nothing here is a new claim.

First made for Depthray, Woodford XR's VR video player. |

"Provided free with no restrictions on use, provided the authors are cited when the data is used in any research or commercial application." Terms in `assets/hrtf/kemar/LICENSE.txt`. |
| ZipDepth licence text | `assets/licenses/zipdepth_LICENSE.txt` | MIT, Copyright (c) 2026 Fabio Tosi. This is the licence of the ZipDepth depth model (a `.tflite` asset handled by the depth lane, not copied here). |

astc-encoder (Apache 2.0), used at build time only; the OpenXR loader is
